Everything you need to know before spending a single naira. Plain English, zero jargon.

Watt vs Watt-Hour

Think of a Watt (W) as your car's speed right now. A Watt-hour (Wh) is how far the tank takes you. A 100W fan running for 10 hours uses 1,000Wh. This is how solar batteries are measured.

Solar Panel

Panels sit on your roof and catch sunlight, turning it into electricity. A 250W panel produces up to 250W in direct sunlight. Nigeria gets 4 to 7 peak sun hours daily, which is excellent.

Inverter

Solar panels and batteries produce DC electricity. Your appliances need AC. The inverter converts DC to AC so everything runs normally. Its wattage is the max load it can handle at once.

Practical Advice

Make Your Solar Work Harder

🌬️
Clean Panels During Harmattan

Dust settles heavily on panels during harmattan and can cut output by up to 25 percent. A simple rinse with clean water every 2 weeks during the dry season recovers all that lost power for free.

❄️
Use Inverter AC Units Only

A regular 1.5HP AC uses up to 1,800W. The same size inverter AC uses 900 to 1,100W. That one switch nearly doubles how long your solar system can run your cooling. Never install regular AC on solar.

💡
Replace Every Bulb With LED

A fluorescent bulb uses 23W. A quality LED bulb gives the same light for 7 to 9W. Across 10 bulbs in your home, that is 140W saved. On solar, every watt saved is more runtime for everything else.

⏱️
Stagger Your High-Watt Appliances

Running your kettle, iron and microwave at the same time can spike above 4,000W instantly. Your inverter may cut out. Use them one at a time and your smaller system handles them all without struggling.

🔋
Never Drain Below 20 Percent

Regularly draining a lithium battery below 20 percent shortens its lifespan significantly. Set your inverter's low-battery cutoff to 20 percent. Your battery will last years longer from this single setting change.

Quick Reference

Solar Glossary

Every solar term you will hear, explained in plain English.

🔍
⚡ Watt (W)

The unit of power. Measures how much electricity something uses at any given moment. A 60W fan uses 60 watts while running.

⏱️ Watt-hour (Wh)

The unit of energy. Measures electricity used over time. Running a 100W fan for 5 hours uses 500Wh of energy from your battery.

📊 Kilowatt-hour (kWh)

1,000 watt-hours. The unit on your NEPA bill. One kWh is what it costs to run a 1,000W appliance for one hour straight.

🔄 Inverter

Converts DC electricity from your battery into AC electricity your home appliances can use. Its wattage rating is the max load it handles at once.

🔋 Battery Capacity

How much energy a battery can store, measured in Ah or Wh. A 200Ah 12V battery holds approximately 2,400Wh of usable energy.

📉 Depth of Discharge (DoD)

The percentage of battery capacity you use before recharging. Most lithium batteries recommend 80 to 90% DoD. Exceeding this regularly shortens battery life.

🎛️ MPPT

Maximum Power Point Tracking. A technology in charge controllers that squeezes maximum energy from your panels at all times.

🚦 Charge Controller

Manages electricity flow from panels to battery. Prevents overcharging and battery damage. MPPT type is significantly more efficient than older PWM type.

🔌 Hybrid Inverter

Combines inverter and MPPT charge controller into one unit. Manages your panels, battery and output automatically. Recommended for full solar installations.

🌞 Solar Panel (PV Module)

Converts sunlight into DC electricity. Rated by wattage output under ideal conditions. Quality panels degrade less than 0.5% per year.

🏠 Load

The total power your appliances consume at a given time, measured in watts. Knowing your load is the starting point for any solar purchase.

🏝️ Off-Grid

A solar setup that operates completely independently from the power grid. Your home runs entirely on solar and battery with zero connection to NEPA.

🎒 Solar Generator

A portable all-in-one unit with battery, inverter and charge controller. No installation needed. Fixed capacity, cannot be expanded.

🔁 Battery Cycle

One full charge and discharge counts as one cycle. Quality lithium batteries handle 2,000 to 3,500 cycles before noticeable capacity loss.

⚗️ LiFePO4 Battery

The safest and most durable lithium battery type. Does not overheat or catch fire like older lithium types. Lasts longer and handles deep discharges better.

🕐 Peak Sun Hours

Hours per day that sunlight is strong enough for panels to operate at full capacity. Most of Nigeria gets 4 to 7 peak sun hours daily, excellent for solar.

Solar 101

Everything you need to know before spending a single naira. Plain English, zero jargon.

⚡
Watt vs Watt-Hour

Think of a Watt (W) as your car's speed right now. A Watt-hour (Wh) is how far the tank takes you. A 100W fan running for 10 hours uses 1,000Wh. This is how solar batteries are measured so you know exactly how long they last.

Start Here
☀️
Solar Panel

Panels sit on your roof and catch sunlight, turning it into electricity. A 250W panel produces up to 250 watts in direct sunlight. Nigeria gets 4 to 6 peak sun hours daily which is excellent for solar generation.

Generation
🔄
Inverter

Solar panels and batteries produce DC electricity. Your appliances need AC. The inverter converts DC to AC so everything runs normally. Its wattage rating is the maximum load it can handle at one time.

Conversion
🔋
Battery

The battery stores energy from your panels during the day so you can use it at night or when it is cloudy. Lithium batteries last longer, charge faster and perform better than older lead-acid types used in most generators.

Storage
🎛️
Charge Controller

Acts like a traffic warden between your panels and battery. It controls how much charge flows in, preventing overcharging which damages batteries. MPPT controllers are the most efficient type and squeeze the most out of your panels.

Protection
🔌
Hybrid Inverter

Combines an inverter and charge controller into one smart unit. It manages your panels, battery and output all at once. The smarter choice for full solar installations because it reduces components and complexity.

Smart Choice
🎒
Solar Generator

Packs a battery, inverter and charge controller into one portable box. Plug in, charge up, use it anywhere. No installation needed. The trade-off is the capacity is fixed and cannot be upgraded if your power needs grow.

Plug and Play
🏝️
Off-Grid Living

Your home runs entirely on its own solar system with zero dependence on NEPA. Panels charge batteries during the day. Batteries power your home at night. No bills, no outages, no noise. Just your own private power supply.

Quick Reference

Solar Glossary

Every solar term you will hear, explained in plain English.

🔍
⚡ Watt (W)

The unit of power. Measures how much electricity something uses at any given moment. A 60W fan uses 60 watts while running.

⏱️ Watt-hour (Wh)

The unit of energy. Measures electricity used over time. Running a 100W fan for 5 hours uses 500Wh of energy from your battery.

📊 Kilowatt-hour (kWh)

1,000 watt-hours. This is the unit on your NEPA bill. One kWh is what it costs to run a 1,000W appliance for one hour straight.

🔄 Inverter

Converts DC electricity from your battery into AC electricity your home appliances can use. Its wattage rating is the max load it handles at once.

🔋 Battery Capacity

How much energy a battery can store, measured in Ah or Wh. A 200Ah 12V battery holds approximately 2,400Wh of usable energy.

📉 Depth of Discharge (DoD)

The percentage of battery capacity you use before recharging. Most lithium batteries recommend 80 to 90% DoD. Exceeding this regularly shortens battery life.

🎛️ MPPT

Maximum Power Point Tracking. A technology in charge controllers and hybrid inverters that squeezes maximum energy from your panels at all times.

🚦 Charge Controller

Manages electricity flow from panels to battery. Prevents overcharging and battery damage. MPPT type is significantly more efficient than older PWM type.

🔌 Hybrid Inverter

Combines inverter and MPPT charge controller into one unit. Manages your panels, battery and output automatically. Recommended for full solar installations.

🌞 Solar Panel (PV Module)

Converts sunlight into DC electricity through photovoltaic cells. Rated by wattage output under ideal conditions. Quality panels degrade less than 0.5% per year.

🏠 Load

The total power your appliances consume at a given time, measured in watts. Knowing your load is the starting point for any solar purchase decision.

🏝️ Off-Grid

A solar setup that operates completely independently from the power grid. Your home runs entirely on solar and battery with zero connection to NEPA.

🎒 Solar Generator

A portable all-in-one unit with battery, inverter and charge controller. No installation needed. Fixed capacity, cannot be expanded. Ideal for smaller loads.

🔁 Battery Cycle

One full charge and discharge counts as one cycle. Quality lithium batteries handle 2,000 to 3,500 cycles before noticeable capacity loss occurs.

⚗️ LiFePO4 Battery

The safest and most durable lithium battery type. Does not overheat or catch fire like older lithium types. Lasts longer and handles deep discharges better.

🕐 Peak Sun Hours

Hours per day that sunlight is strong enough for panels to operate at full rated capacity. Most of Nigeria gets 4 to 6 peak sun hours daily, excellent for solar.

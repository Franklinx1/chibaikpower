Our Story

Built for Nigeria.
By Nigerians.

No showrooms. No bots. No pressure. Just honest solar advice and the tools to make the right decision for your home.

We started Chibaik Power because of one frustrating experience most Nigerians have had: you walk into a solar shop, describe your situation, and they recommend the most expensive system they have in stock. No calculation. No education. Just a quote.

We believed Nigerians deserved better. You deserve to walk into any solar conversation already knowing your numbers — your load, your battery requirements, your panel needs — so nobody can oversell you.

So we built the tools we wished existed. A real load calculator that takes your actual appliances and tells you exactly what size system you need. A fuel cost calculator that shows you what your generator is actually costing you over time. Educational content written in plain English, not technical brochure language.

We source equipment from trusted suppliers across Nigeria and deliver anywhere in the country. When you chat us, you speak directly to a human being who will give you an honest recommendation — even if that recommendation is that you don't need what you think you need.

This site will always be free. The tools will always be free. The advice will always be honest.

Forget NEPA. Forever.
